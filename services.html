What we offer

Our Services

Founded in 2024 by a group of local activists and retired legal practitioners, the Mount Frere Siyakhana Legal Advice Centre was built to address the critical gap in legal aid and social services across rural areas in the Eastern Cape, operating in the Alfred Nzo District.

Services We Provide

Accessible Legal Aid Resources

We help people get the legal support they need during difficult times, providing free advice and guidance on a range of matters.

Community Support Services

Enable online consultation bookings for one-on-one services available on weekdays. We work closely with local communities.

Facilitate Donor Funding

We assist community members and partners in accessing funding opportunities that support essential health and social procedures.

Siyakhana Legal Advice Centre provides professional legal advice and representation to those who cannot afford it. We try to help as many people as possible, including vulnerable groups such as women, children, the elderly, disabled, and the rural poor.
