About Siyakhana

Learning about our history, mission and vision for the rural communities of the Alfred Nzo District.

History of our Organisation

Founded in 2024 by a group of local activists and retired legal practitioners, the Mount Frere Siyakhana Legal Advice Centre was built to address the critical gap in legal aid and social services across rural areas in the Eastern Cape. Operating in the Alfred Nzo District, the centre began as a small drop-in consultation desk assisting elderly community members with pension and SASSA grant applications.

Mission Statement

To provide legal services, human rights education, and social support to the rural community of Mount Frere. By offering free legal advice, mediation, and administrative assistance with social services, we aim to empower those who need it most.

Vision Statement

To build an empowered and self-sustaining community in the Alfred Nzo District where human rights are upheld, access to justice is free and accessible to everyone, and every person has the knowledge and resources to advocate for their rights.

Follow us on

Siyakhana Legal Advice Centre provides professional legal advice and representation to those who cannot afford it. We try to help as many people as possible, including vulnerable groups such as women, children, the elderly, disabled, and the rural poor.
